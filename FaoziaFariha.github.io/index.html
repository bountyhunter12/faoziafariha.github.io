About

I am completing my B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering at the Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), graduating in June 2026 with a cumulative GPA of 3.84/4.00. My thesis, GreenFed-LoRA, supervised by Professor Mohammad Shamsul Arefin, designs a federated fine-tuning framework for large language models that treats energy consumption and carbon emissions as core optimisation objectives alongside accuracy.

I am currently a Research Intern at ELITE Research Lab (United States, remote), where I investigate AI agent security, with a focus on secure cross-session memory protection for LLM-based agents. I am also a Graduate Research Member at the NLP Lab, CUET, where I have contributed to three peer-reviewed shared-task papers at SemEval-2026 and LT-EDI-2025, including a multimodal misogyny-detection system that placed 6th worldwide.

My broader interest is in making large language models work well outside their best-resourced settings: efficient and sustainable fine-tuning through federated learning, multimodal understanding, and NLP for low-resource languages like Bangla. I am looking to continue this work through research collaborations and graduate study, and I am happy to hear from anyone working on related problems.

Research Interests

Green AI & Federated Learning

Energy-aware federated fine-tuning of large language models: treating carbon emissions and communication overhead as first-class objectives, not afterthoughts, alongside task accuracy.

NLP for Low-Resource Languages

Instruction-tuned LLMs and few-shot prompting for Bangla and multilingual tasks: psycholinguistic marker extraction, conspiracy detection, joke generation under lexical constraints, and reasoning over Bangla physics questions.

Multimodal AI & AI Safety

Fusing text and vision encoders for tasks like misogyny detection, and, more recently, threat modelling for persistent memory vulnerabilities in multi-agent LLM pipelines.
